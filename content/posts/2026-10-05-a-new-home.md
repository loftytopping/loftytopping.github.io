My old personal page had not been touched since 2016, which says a lot about how academic careers
fill up with other things. This site replaces it.

You will find an overview of the areas I work in, an up-to-date CV, and links to my publications
and software. I will also use it to post the occasional piece of writing and news, and to list the
events and conferences I will be at, so if you would like to meet up somewhere, check the events page.
