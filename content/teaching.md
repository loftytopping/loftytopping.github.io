## Summer schools and short courses

I can run intensive courses from a single day to two weeks, in person or online, for PhD cohorts,
early-career researchers, professional staff or mixed audiences. Each course is hands-on: participants
work through real environmental data and models rather than toy examples, and leave with code they can
reuse. Courses can be combined into a longer summer school, and I am happy to tailor the content and
level to a programme's needs.

Topics I offer include:

- **Python for environmental scientists.** Programming from scratch, building modular code and
  visualisation, for people who have never coded or want to code properly.
- **Machine learning for environmental data.** Supervised and unsupervised methods, emulation of
  process models, explainable machine learning, and time series and spatial data.
- **Large language models and AI systems.** How language models work, retrieval-augmented generation,
  agentic tools and how to evaluate them, taught with interactive demonstrations that run in the browser
  with nothing to install.
- **Aerosol and atmospheric process modelling.** From theory to working code, following my textbook
  [Introduction to Aerosol Modelling](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781119625728).

Past delivery includes the annual modelling and data analysis training module for the EPSRC Aerosol &
Droplet Science Centre for Doctoral Training since 2019, NERC and NVIDIA short courses on parallel and
GPU programming, and aerosol physics teaching at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (2015-2019).

If you are organising a summer school or training programme, email me with the audience, length and
dates you have in mind.

## University teaching

At the University of Manchester I teach across undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in the
Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences.

- **Natural Scientist's Toolkit.** First-year programming, maths and physics for around 140 students;
  I lead both the programming component and the maths and physics component.
- **Data Science for Air Quality.** Course lead on the MSc Pollution and Environmental Control.
- **MSc Data Science, Earth and Environmental Analytics pathway.** I initiated and developed this
  pathway and supervise dissertation projects.
- **Research supervision.** PhD and Masters projects in modelling, machine learning and AI systems.
  See [Work with me](work-with-me.html).

## International partnerships

I work with universities and organisations around the world on research, training and education, and I
am keen to build new partnerships. That can mean joint training programmes and summer schools,
co-supervised PhD students, staff and student exchanges, or joint research proposals. Current links
include an adjunct professorship at Zhejiang University and an advisory role with the Digital Solutions
programme at Hong Kong Baptist University. If your institution is interested in working together,
please get in touch.
